1. Who we are

Kelime Köprüsü is published by [PUBLISHER NAME / COMPANY] (“we”). Contact: [EMAIL] · Address: [ADDRESS].

2. In short

  • No account is needed. If you wish, you can sign in with your Google account (section 4, “Account”).
  • If you do not sign in, your progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.
  • Gameplay statistics are collected only if you allow it (section 4, “Gameplay statistics”); otherwise none are sent.
  • When advertising and in-app purchases are enabled, the third parties listed in Section 4 may process data.

3. Information stored on your device

Game progress (chapter, score, streak, review list, learned words), an unfinished puzzle, coin balance and hint charges, settings (sound, music), whether the ad-free version was purchased and a daily reward counter. We cannot access this information. It is deleted from the device when you delete the app or use Settings → “Reset progress”. On iPhone it is kept in the storage iOS assigns to the app. If iCloud is on, game progress (including coins) is also backed up to your own iCloud account (Apple’s iCloud key-value store) so it can be restored on another device with the same Apple ID. This backup stays with Apple in your account; we cannot access it. You can turn iCloud off in iPhone Settings.

4. Third parties

Account: Google sign-in and account backup (Google Firebase) — only if you sign in

You can sign in with your Google account in Settings → Account. Signing in is optional; every feature of the game works without it. If you sign in, the following is stored in Google’s Firebase service:

  • Account data (Firebase Authentication): your Google account name, email address, profile photo address, the user ID assigned to you, account creation and last sign-in times;
  • Game backup (Cloud Firestore): the game progress listed in section 3 (including coins), a randomly generated ID for the device that made the backup, and the backup time.

This is used only to back up your progress to your account and restore it when you sign in with the same account on another device (iPhone or web). It is not used for advertising or any other purpose and is not shared with anyone. Game backups are stored on Google’s servers in Europe; the sign-in service runs on Google’s global infrastructure (including the US). Backups can be accessed only with your own account.

Sign out ends the session on this device; your account and backups remain. Delete account (Settings → Account) immediately deletes your account and all backups in it; the progress on your device stays. Copies in Google’s backup systems are deleted on Google’s own schedule. Details: Firebase privacy and security.

Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) — only with your permission

To improve the game we collect anonymous gameplay statistics only with your explicit permission. You are asked on first launch and can give or withdraw permission at any time in Settings → Statistics. Without permission the analytics tool is not even loaded.

Only gameplay events are collected:

  • a puzzle being started, finished or left; chapter, topic, number of words, duration, score;
  • which help (Meaning, Sentence, Synonyms, Reveal letter) was used; coins earned and spent;
  • finishing or skipping the tutorial, chapter progress, daily goal, which screens were opened;
  • technical data processed by the service itself: a random ID for this installation, device model, OS version, app version, approximate location (country/city, estimated from the IP address; the IP address is not stored), session length.

Your name, email address, account ID and the answers you type are not sent, and statistics are not linked to your account. No advertising identifier (IDFA) is used, there is no cross-app tracking, and ad personalisation and Google signals are off. On the web, Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga) only after you allow it; they are deleted when you withdraw permission. Details: Firebase privacy and security.

Apple (App Store and in-app purchases)

Downloads and in-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details; the device only receives confirmation that a purchase succeeded. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob) [TO BE FINALISED BEFORE ADS ARE ENABLED]

The app shows two kinds of ads: (1) an interstitial ad at the end of a chapter, at most once every three puzzles, to users who have not bought the ad-free version; (2) rewarded ads that users may choose to start to earn coins (also available, as an option, to ad-free owners). Ads are provided by Google. Non-personalised ads are requested and App Tracking Transparency permission is not requested. To serve ads and operate its service, Google may process: IP address (to estimate approximate location), device identifiers, ads shown and interactions with them, usage information such as app launches, and crash and performance data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Pronunciation

The speaker button reads words aloud with your device’s speech engine. On iPhone this happens on the device. Some web browsers may use their own online speech service.

Web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep technical logs such as IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
